  depends on the first, not the second:

              second-chance. This is a basketball rate (~5-12%), and it
              should be roughly flat across the flag-complete era.
  A season where present is high but true is 0 would mean the flag is
  carried but never set — a silent defect the Week 5 report could not
  have distinguished from a genuinely low fastbreak rate.
